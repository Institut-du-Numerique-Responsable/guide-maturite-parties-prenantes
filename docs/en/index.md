# Stakeholder maturity guide for Sustainable IT

**Evaluate and influence the Sustainable IT maturity of your stakeholders (suppliers, service providers, partners).**

Public report of the workgroup of the [Institut du Numérique Responsable](https://institutnr.org) (INR) and the European Institutes for Sustainable IT (ISIT).

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

- **10 families of criteria**, from governance to societal impacts
- **48 open questions**, each with its rationale
- **Weighted criteria** (P1 to P3, 100 points per chapter)
- **Bilingual** French / English, licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0

</div>

## Where to start?

1. Read the [synthesis](synthesis.md) and [how to use this guide](how-to-use.md).
2. Pick the chapters relevant to your stakeholders, from [governance](chapters/01-governance-strategy.md) to [societal impacts](chapters/10-societal-impacts.md).
3. Send the **questionnaire** (open questions and rationales) to stakeholders; keep the **evaluation criteria** for analysing the answers and for the debriefing.
4. Score the answers with the Excel grid, which computes the maturity score per chapter.

## Contribute

The guide evolves with feedback from its users. INR members and anyone interested can propose a correction or an improvement on the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Institut-du-Numerique-Responsable/guide-maturite-parties-prenantes): every page of this site has an "Edit" link.
